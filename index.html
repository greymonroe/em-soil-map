2 · Where did it come from?

How to get it from Google Maps
  1. Open Google Maps and find the spot where you dug the soil.
  2. Press and hold on that exact spot until a red pin drops.
  3. The numbers show up in the search box at the top (like 38.53920, -121.75060).
  4. Tap them to copy, come back here, and paste into the box above.

The second number is negative in California. Keep the minus sign.

4 · Look, feel, smell

Wet a bit of soil until it's like putty, then work through the questions.

A. Squeeze it in your fist. Does it stay in a ball?
B. Push it out between thumb and finger. How long a ribbon before it breaks?
C. Wet a pinch in your palm and rub. How does it feel?

5 · Chemistry (after it settles)

Do the tests your group's kits have. For each one: pick the kit type, match the color to the chart, enter the number if the chart shows one, and snap a photo of the result next to the chart. Skip any test you didn't do.

What it means
